AI Frameworks in Modern War

Strategic Deterrence & AI-Enabled Defensive Capabilities

Deterrence Frameworks How AI Enables
Cheap Physical Hardware (Attritable Mass) 1000’s of weapons per strike
AI Essential to tie weapon mass together-for example 1000’s of drones
Operational Mechanism: Attritable Mass Swarming Technical Breakdown

AI allows 100’s to1000’s of low-cost platforms to communicate via self-healing Mobile Ad-hoc Networks. AI instantly reassigns tasks across the remaining swarm without human intervention. Helps attacks and defense. Can lose 80% and still succeed (Attritable weapons)

Immense volumes of sensor data (Satellite, Radar, Acoustic Sensors)
AI Consolidates and Processes Targeting data at lightning speed
Operational Mechanism: Rapid Sensor Fusion

AI systems ingest and filter noise, spot camouflaged troop movements, and generate verified targeting coordinates in milliseconds rather than hours. AI deprives an adversary of the time window needed to launch a successful surprise attack.

Highly Disruptive Infrastructure Targeting
AI Identifies High-Value Targets to inflict maximum damage and defeat Air Defenses
Operational Mechanism: Cost Imposition & Flight Routing

AI calculates optimal terrain-masking flight paths for long-range autonomous strike weapons and identifies points of failure in target’s industrial grid. This guarantees that strikes inflict severe, non-linear economic damage.

Directed Energy Defense Shields-“Infinite Magazine” weapons
AI Essential to High-Energy Lasers and High Power Microwave defensive systems which target and kill or disable via electronic disruption
Operational Mechanism: Microsecond Beam Slewing

Computers track incoming saturation swarms at microsecond intervals. The AI calculates target prioritization slews the laser beam or microwave emitter from target to target faster than any human operator could acquire sight lines.

Electronic Jamming Resistant Weapons
AI replaces external navigation signals with onboard, autonomous spatial awareness
Operational Mechanism: Terminal Optical Computer Vision

Edge AI vision models allow uncrewed systems to navigate without GPS. During the terminal phase of flight, onboard computer vision recognizes target shapes without human tele-operation or external radio frequencies, making electronic jamming ineffective.

Coordination of Coalitions/Allies
AI combines data from allied nations using different hardware and formats
Operational Mechanism: Protocol Translation & Cyber Defense

AI serves as data translator, creating a unified operational picture across allied forces. Autonomous AI agents also detect, and isolate cyber intrusion attempts to prevent disruption of joint command operations.
